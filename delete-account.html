Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

In the app Open Moora → Settings → Account → Delete account → confirm.

Your account and associated data will be scheduled for deletion immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by email:

Send an email to support@moora.ink
Subject: Account deletion request
Please include: the email address you registered with

We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your account record (email address, display name)
  • Your practice data: mood notes, chosen writing phrases, session records, preferences
  • Your Ink Memory timeline and generated insights
  • Any photos you uploaded to the app
  • Your subscription linkage records

What may be retained

  • Purchase and transaction records — retained by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes. These records are not controlled by us.
  • Anonymised, aggregated statistics that no longer identify you.
  • Records we are legally required to keep under applicable law.

How long it takes

Deletion is processed within 30 days of a verified request. Backup copies may persist for a short additional period before being overwritten.

删除你的账号

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 2 日

应用：Moora
开发者：杭州字在科技有限公司
联系邮箱：support@moora.ink

方式一：在应用内删除

应用内操作 打开 Moora → 设置 → 账号 → 删除账号 → 确认。

删除后，你的账号及关联数据将立即进入删除流程。

方式二：不装应用也能申请删除

若你已卸载应用，可通过邮件申请删除：

发送邮件至 support@moora.ink
邮件标题：账号删除申请
请注明：你注册时使用的邮箱地址

我们会在核实身份后 30 天内完成删除。

会被删除的数据

  • 账号记录（邮箱地址、昵称）
  • 练习数据：心情笔记、选择的书写词句、练习记录、偏好设置
  • 墨迹记忆时间轴与生成的洞察内容
  • 你上传到应用的照片
  • 订阅关联记录

可能保留的数据

  • 购买与交易记录：由 Google Play 与 RevenueCat 依税务、会计与反欺诈要求保留，不受我们控制。
  • 匿名化、聚合后的统计数据：已无法识别到你个人。
  • 法律要求必须保留的记录。

处理时间

核实申请后 30 天内完成删除。备份副本可能在短期内继续存在，随后被覆盖清除。
