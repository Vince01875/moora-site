1. Who we are

Moora ("Moora", "we", "us") is a calligraphy and mindfulness app operated by Hangzhou Zizai Technology Co., Ltd., a company registered in Hangzhou, China. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and display name, used for authentication through Supabase Auth.

Practice data

  • Mood notes, the writing phrases you choose, session duration, background sound selection, and how you feel after each session. This data builds your personal Ink Memory.
  • Writing tool preferences (pen, brush, or both), used to personalise phrase recommendations.

Photos (optional)

  • If you choose to upload a photo of your calligraphy work, it is stored securely and linked only to your account.

Purchase information

  • Subscription status for Moora Plus. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing and managed through RevenueCat. We never receive or store your full card details.

Technical and usage data

  • User ID, anonymous analytics identifier, device and app information, and app activity events such as page views, writing sessions, recommendations and AI usage events.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, health data, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising SDKs.

3. How we use your information

  • Providing app features — generating writing prompts, displaying your Ink Memory timeline, and producing personal insights so you can compare your practice over time.
  • AI processing — necessary writing context or artwork images may be sent to OpenAI or Volcano Engine Doubao (火山引擎豆包) in order to generate writing content, companion conversation and artwork analysis. We do not include your name or email address in these prompts.
  • Subscriptions — processing, verifying and restoring purchases through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.
  • Analytics — understanding feature usage in order to improve the app. Our analytics is first-party; we use no third-party behavioural analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Account management and support — responding to your requests and maintaining your account.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

We process personal data on the basis of: performance of a contract (providing the app you signed up for), legitimate interests (improving the app and preventing abuse), and consent (where we ask for it, such as optional photo upload).

5. Sharing with third parties

We share data only with service providers necessary to run Moora:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • OpenAI, Volcano Engine Doubao — AI processing of writing context or artwork images.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status management.
  • Google Play — payment processing.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising purposes.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL) on servers located in the United States. Photos are stored in Supabase Storage. We use Row Level Security so that only you can access your own records. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and associated data at any time by any of the following methods:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account
  • On the web: https://www.moora.ink/delete-account.html
  • By email: support@moora.ink

We will delete your data within 30 days of receiving your request.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, email support@moora.ink.

9. Children's privacy

Moora is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email.

11. Contact

1. 关于我们

Moora（以下简称"我们"）是一款书法与正念练习应用，由杭州字在科技有限公司运营。本政策说明我们收集哪些信息、为什么收集，以及你可以做出的选择。

2. 我们收集的信息

账号信息

  • 邮箱地址与昵称，用于通过 Supabase Auth 完成身份验证。

练习数据

  • 心情笔记、你选择的书写词句、练习时长、背景音选择，以及练习后的感受。这些内容用于建立你的个人「墨迹记忆（Ink Memory）」。
  • 书写工具偏好（硬笔、毛笔或两者皆有），用于个性化词句推荐。

照片（选填）

  • 若你选择上传书写作品照片，照片会被安全储存，并仅与你的账号关联。

购买信息

  • Moora Plus 订阅状态。付款通过 Google Play Billing 处理，并由 RevenueCat 管理。我们不会取得或储存你的完整付款卡资料。

技术数据与使用数据

  • 用户 ID、匿名分析标识、设备与应用信息，以及页面浏览、书写、推荐、AI 使用等应用活动事件。

我们不收集精确位置、通讯录、健康数据或广告标识符。应用内没有广告 SDK。

3. 我们如何使用信息

  • 提供应用功能：生成书写内容、展示墨迹记忆时间轴、生成阶段性洞察，协助你比较不同时期的练习。
  • AI 处理：必要的书写脉络或作品影像可能发送至 OpenAI 或火山引擎豆包，用于生成书写内容、对话与作品分析。我们不会在提示中主动加入你的姓名或邮箱。
  • 订阅管理：通过 Google Play Billing 与 RevenueCat 处理、验证及恢复订阅。
  • 数据分析：了解功能使用情况以改进产品。我们使用自建分析，未接入第三方行为分析或广告 SDK。
  • 账号管理与客服：回应你的请求并维护账号。

4. 与第三方共享

  • Supabase：数据库、身份验证与文件储存。
  • OpenAI、火山引擎豆包：对书写脉络或作品影像进行 AI 处理。
  • RevenueCat：订阅状态管理。
  • Google Play：支付处理。

我们不会出售、出租或为广告目的分享你的个人数据。

5. 数据存储位置

你的数据安全储存于 Supabase（PostgreSQL），服务器位于美国。照片储存于 Supabase Storage。我们使用行级安全策略（Row Level Security），确保只有你能访问自己的数据。传输过程全程使用 HTTPS 加密。

6. 数据保留与删除

我们会在你的账号存续期间保留数据。你可以随时通过以下任一方式删除账号及关联数据：

  • 应用内：设置 → 账号 → 删除账号
  • 网页：https://www.moora.ink/delete-account.html
  • 邮件：support@moora.ink

我们会在收到请求后 30 天内完成删除。
